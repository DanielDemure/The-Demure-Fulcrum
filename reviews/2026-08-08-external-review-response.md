# Author Response to the 8 August 2026 External Review

**Document status:** public response to supplied review material  
**Framework status:** working conceptual paper — not peer reviewed

## Purpose

The supplied review argues for a clearer typology, falsifiable predictions, stronger scholarly references, explicit treatment of Polyvagal Theory controversy, a limitations section, more careful positioning of Hexure, an explanation of the name, and broader cross-cultural coverage.

Many of those concerns were already addressed by the August 2026 scholarly reconstruction. This response records what is accepted, what is modified, and what is not adopted so that the repository does not silently oscillate between incompatible versions of the theory.

## Correction: the supplied review separated fMRI and EEG

An earlier version of this response described the rejected proposal as “fMRI within 500 ms.” That summary was inaccurate. The supplied review used **fMRI for spatial activation and connectivity hypotheses** and **EEG for the separate 300–500 ms expertise and timing hypothesis**. The distinction between modalities is methodologically valid and is now recorded correctly.

The repository still does not predeclare a unique dlPFC/vmPFC/amygdala pattern, cortisol profile, or EEG time window as characteristic of a new response system. Those questions remain secondary to reliable behavioural definition and control conditions matched for language, effort, threat, and social interaction.

## Why the central framing remains narrower

The strongest defensible distinction is functional and level-specific:

- a first-order response selects an action within the current situation;
- a negotiation meta-response attempts, through an influenceable counterpart or process, to alter options, information, commitments, timing, participants, enforcement, or payoffs.

This distinction can be tested without claiming a dedicated neural module. It also permits failure: the framework loses distinctiveness if the behaviour cannot be coded reliably, does not respond selectively to negotiability, or adds no explanatory value beyond active coping plus negotiation skill.

## Four-form typology

The review usefully separates four recurring meanings. The repository adopts them with different evidentiary status:

1. **Interpersonal negotiation — core:** contingent exchange between identifiable people or groups.
2. **Institutional negotiation — core:** contingent use of authorised procedures, representatives, appeals, rules, or multi-actor systems to alter terms.
3. **Intrapersonal negotiation — conceptual extension:** self-regulation among competing goals, identities, values, or temporal selves.
4. **Existential negotiation — phenomenological extension:** bargaining with fate, God, death, meaning, or an imagined absolute.

The last two are not assumed to share the same mechanisms as bargaining with a responsive external actor.

## Evidence sequencing

The review proposes ambitious neuroimaging, clinical, developmental, ethnographic, and simulation studies. The repository adopts a staged order:

1. construct definition and difficult-case interviews;
2. behavioural codebook and inter-rater reliability;
3. experimental manipulation of negotiability;
4. convergent, discriminant, and incremental validity;
5. field and cross-cultural measurement;
6. only then psychophysiology, EEG, endocrine measures, or neuroimaging.

This order reduces the risk of assigning biological specificity to an unstable concept.

## Cross-cultural standard

A cultural example should not be counted merely because it contains reconciliation, consensus, or reciprocity. Comparative work should document:

- who has authority to change terms;
- whether interests are represented individually or collectively;
- how contingency is expressed, including indirect or mediated forms;
- what constitutes a credible commitment;
- whether refusal, appeal, delay, exit, or collective voice remains possible;
- whose agency and safety are preserved;
- whether the practice is common, exceptional, idealised, historical, or contemporary.

Failure of measurement invariance would require localisation or revision rather than forcing every practice into one Western category.

## Hexure standard

`Rewiring Engine`, `Sanctioned Small World`, and `Cathedral Building` remain project concepts. Network science can motivate hypotheses about partner choice, reputation, bridging, commitment, and reinvestment; it does not validate those branded terms. Any simulation or pilot must specify competing models, rules, outcomes, adverse effects, robustness checks, and conditions for rejection.

## Resulting repository changes

This review cycle adds:

- an explicit four-form typology;
- secondary extension hypotheses with prerequisites and disconfirmation conditions;
- a cross-cultural research matrix;
- a preregistration outline and standalone formal protocol for Hexure-inspired simulations;
- eight named limitations including single-author origin;
- a correction distinguishing the review's fMRI spatial proposal from its EEG temporal proposal;
- a scalable SVG README banner, updated review questions, navigation, changelog, CI path tracking, and repository validation.

The guiding principle is simple: criticism should make the framework more precise and more vulnerable to disconfirmation—not merely more impressive-sounding.
