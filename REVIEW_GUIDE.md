# Review Guide

The Demure Fulcrum is a **working conceptual paper, not a peer-reviewed or empirically validated theory**. Critical review is invited, especially where the framework duplicates existing constructs, makes an invalid inference, or fails under realistic power asymmetry.

## Highest-priority questions

1. **Construct distinctiveness**  
   Does strict negotiation under threat describe something meaningfully narrower than problem-focused coping plus ordinary negotiation skill? Which existing construct best explains the same behaviour?

2. **Operational boundary**  
   Are the seven inclusion criteria sufficient to distinguish negotiation from persuasion, assertiveness, appeasement, mediation, affiliation, and self-regulation? Which difficult cases break the definition?

3. **Formal model**  
   Does the distinction between selecting an action in `G` and attempting to transform `G` into `G′` improve explanation, or merely redescribe bargaining in new language?

4. **Falsifiability**  
   Are the predictions and disconfirmation criteria capable of producing a genuine negative result? What result should force abandonment rather than minor revision?

5. **Power and ethics**  
   Does the agency language adequately handle coercion, trauma, poverty, disability, institutional exclusion, and situations where negotiation is unsafe? Where does the framework risk blaming people for structural constraint?

6. **Cross-cultural validity**  
   Is the definition tied to explicit, individualist, Western bargaining conventions? How should implicit, relational, collective, or mediator-led practices be represented?

7. **Evidence calibration**  
   Which claims are supported by the cited literature, which are only plausible inferences, and which require direct study?

## How to review

Open a GitHub issue with one primary claim per issue. Please include:

- the section and exact sentence or construct being challenged;
- the type of concern: definition, evidence, logic, ethics, measurement, source, or presentation;
- a proposed correction or a source that creates the conflict;
- whether the concern affects the core construct or only an application.

Strong negative reviews are useful. The project should become narrower or be rejected if evidence does not support distinctiveness.

## Review scope

The core working paper and three research companions are the main scholarly scope. [`applications/hexure.md`](applications/hexure.md) is a speculative application and should not be treated as evidence for the framework.

## Author response standard

Responses should classify feedback as:

- **accepted** — change will be made;
- **partially accepted** — concern is valid but proposed remedy is modified;
- **not accepted** — response explains the evidentiary or conceptual reason;
- **requires evidence** — issue becomes an explicit research question.

No criticism should be dismissed merely because it challenges the name or central intuition.
