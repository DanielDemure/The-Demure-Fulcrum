# The Demure Fulcrum — Full Scholarly Revision Design

**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Author:** Daniel Demure  
**Working branch:** `revision/agency-meta-response`  
**Status:** Approved direction; implementation pending final review of this design

## 1. Purpose

This revision will turn the repository from a broad literary-academic argument for a fourth biological stress instinct into a transparent, testable conceptual framework and research agenda.

The central idea will be preserved but stated more precisely:

> **The Demure Fulcrum proposes negotiation as an agency-preserving, higher-order meta-response under threat. Rather than merely selecting an action within the existing situation, the actor attempts to alter the situation's constraints, options, information, commitments, or payoffs.**

The revised paper will **not** claim that negotiation is already proven to be a fourth autonomic reflex or a distinct hard-wired neural system equivalent to fight, flight, or freeze. It will specify what evidence would be required to support stronger versions of the claim.

## 2. Chosen approach

Three approaches were considered:

1. **Minimal correction:** retain the current paper and soften isolated claims. This is fast but leaves the construct-definition, evidence, flow, and reference problems largely intact.
2. **Full scholarly reconstruction:** rewrite the core paper around a precise meta-response construct, move applications outside the evidentiary core, rebuild the references, and add falsifiable predictions. **Chosen.**
3. **Immediate two-paper programme:** publish one conceptual paper and one application paper at once. This offers the cleanest long-term separation but would force an immature Hexure application into premature publication.

The selected approach produces one strong conceptual paper plus clearly labelled companion documents. Hexure remains available as an application hypothesis, but it will no longer be presented as evidence validating the psychological framework.

## 3. Scope

### In scope

- Rewrite the main academic paper in full.
- Replace the primary-instinct claim with the higher-order meta-response formulation.
- Define negotiation operationally and state boundary conditions.
- Distinguish interpersonal negotiation from intrapersonal and existential extensions.
- Distinguish negotiation from fight, flight, freeze, appeasement/fawn, tend-and-befriend, cognitive reappraisal, problem-focused coping, assertiveness, social approach, and ordinary bargaining skill.
- Add a formal game-transformation account: action selection within game `G` versus attempts to transform `G` into `G′`.
- Add testable predictions, rival explanations, disconfirmation criteria, and a staged empirical research programme.
- Remove obsolete triune-brain or “reptilian brain” language.
- Treat Polyvagal Theory as a contested optional interpretation, not a foundation.
- Reclassify cultural material as interpretive and phenomenological illustration, not empirical validation.
- Move Hexure, trading, strategic-neutrality doctrine, OMAD, radical transparency, the Knight's Oath, and Cathedral Building out of the evidentiary core.
- Replace weak and irrelevant references with a smaller set of primary studies, scholarly books, and high-quality reviews.
- Add repository metadata and review infrastructure.
- Regenerate the PDF reproducibly from the revised source.

### Out of scope

- Claiming empirical validation that has not occurred.
- Inventing unique biomarkers, hormonal profiles, brain regions, or sub-second fMRI predictions.
- Conducting a human-subject experiment.
- Publishing a peer-reviewed journal article or representing the repository as peer reviewed.
- Claiming that all cultures or all conscious beings use the construct identically.
- Creating a mature scientific validation of Hexure from the existing twenty-trade anecdote.
- Automatically merging the revision branch into `main`.

## 4. Conceptual architecture

### 4.1 Levels of analysis

The paper will distinguish four levels that the current version partly conflates:

1. **Physiological state:** autonomic arousal and regulation.
2. **Action tendency or defensive behaviour:** confrontation, escape, immobility, appeasement, affiliation, and approach.
3. **Higher-order response policy:** appraisal, planning, information seeking, conditional exchange, and attempts to restructure the threat situation.
4. **Institutional or strategic application:** mediation systems, network design, Hexure, and ethical doctrine.

The Demure Fulcrum belongs primarily at level 3. Evidence from levels 1 and 2 may constrain or support it, but does not by itself establish a distinct biological response system. Level 4 is downstream application and cannot validate level 3 without independent study.

### 4.2 Core construct

A response counts as **negotiation in the strict sense** when all of the following are present:

- a threat, conflict, constraint, or meaningful adverse prospect;
- an identifiable counterpart or influenceable institutional process;
- partial interdependence between actors;
- communication or signalling;
- a proposal, counterproposal, conditional concession, information exchange, or commitment;
- an attempt to alter available options, constraints, obligations, or payoffs;
- and some retained capacity for refusal, counterproposal, delay, exit, or alternative action.

Negotiation may fail, be distributive, be manipulative, or occur under severe power asymmetry. It is therefore not defined as automatically ethical, cooperative, calm, or win-win.

### 4.3 Extensions

- **Interpersonal or institutional negotiation** is the core construct and the primary empirical target.
- **Intrapersonal negotiation** is a related self-regulatory process and will be described as a conceptual extension, not assumed to be identical to interpersonal bargaining.
- **Existential negotiation** describes the lived or narrative experience of bargaining with fate, God, death, identity, or meaning. It will be presented as a phenomenological and cultural extension, not direct neurobiological evidence.

### 4.4 Agency

Agency will be defined behaviourally rather than rhetorically. Relevant indicators include representing one's interests, preserving conditional choice, generating alternatives, seeking information, making counterproposals, and maintaining a veto, exit, delay, or fallback option where feasible.

Agency is not absolute. Appeasement can be an intelligent survival response where refusal is dangerous or impossible. The paper will avoid blaming victims or treating short-term submission as proof of personal failure.

## 6. Testability and empirical programme

The paper will include at least four preregisterable predictions:

1. **Negotiability effect:** strict negotiation behaviour should increase when an influenceable counterpart, communication channel, partial interdependence, leverage, and deliberation time are available.
2. **Incremental validity:** a Demure Fulcrum measure must explain behaviour beyond problem-focused coping, perceived control, assertiveness, cognitive reappraisal, social approach, and prior negotiation skill.
3. **Negotiation–appeasement dissociation:** negotiation should be associated with counterproposals, conditional concessions, information seeking, and preserved alternatives; appeasement should more often involve unilateral concessions aimed at immediate threat reduction.
4. **Stress-regulation interaction:** stress should not have a simple deterministic effect. Appraisal, controllability, preparation, and regulation should moderate whether an actor can engage in negotiation.

Disconfirmation criteria will be explicit. The distinctiveness claim is weakened if negotiation does not form a coherent construct, does not respond selectively to negotiability conditions, or adds no explanatory value beyond established constructs.

The staged research programme will begin with construct definition and behavioural experiments, not neuroimaging. Physiological or neural work will only be proposed after behavioural validity is established.

## 7. Evidence and citation policy

### Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary empirical studies and original theoretical publications.
2. Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and major scholarly syntheses.
3. Academic books and reference works where primary literature is not the appropriate source.
4. Official institutional sources for repository, licensing, and archival facts.
5. Popular sources only when the popular artefact itself is the object of cultural analysis.

Wikipedia, Investopedia, commercial therapy sites, Reddit, Pinterest, Facebook, TikTok, generic film lists, and promotional blogs will not be used as scientific support.

### Claim–evidence discipline

- Every empirical claim must have a source capable of supporting that exact claim.
- Author-origin claims will cite Daniel Demure's working paper or provenance note, not be treated as independent validation.
- Cultural examples will cite primary works where feasible and will be labelled illustrative.
- Contested subjects, especially Polyvagal Theory and the fawn construct, will include the nature and limits of the controversy.
- The bibliography will be stored in `references/references.bib` and rendered into the Markdown/PDF workflow.

## 8. Companion-document architecture

### `research/01_threat_response_and_regulation.md`

A concise scholarly review of defensive responses, appraisal, controllability, regulation, and socially mediated responses. It will replace the current advocacy-oriented literature review.

### `research/02_construct_and_formal_model.md`

The expanded construct definition, taxonomy, formal `G → G′` model, rival hypotheses, measurement ideas, and experimental designs.

### `research/03_cultural_and_philosophical_illustrations.md`

A clearly labelled interpretive companion covering myth, literature, cinema, and existential bargaining. It will explain what such material can and cannot evidence.

### `applications/hexure.md`

A separate speculative application note. It may retain network rewiring, strategic intermediation, Cathedral Building, and the Knight's Oath, but must distinguish:

- descriptive claims;
- ethical commitments;
- hypotheses;
- anecdotes;
- and validated evidence.

The trading example will not be called empirical or quantitative validation. If retained, it will be labelled a non-generalizable personal case note with no causal inference.

### `provenance/README.md`

Records the origin and evolution of the concept, including the 2025 foundational notes, without using those notes as independent evidence.

## 11. Build and PDF strategy

The Markdown file is the source of truth. A reproducible build script will generate the PDF using available open tooling, with a clear failure message when dependencies are absent.

The generated PDF must:

- match the revised Markdown's title, abstract, headings, tables, references, and status;
- contain selectable text;
- include page numbers and readable margins;
- avoid clipped tables and broken links;
- carry the working-paper/not-peer-reviewed notice;
- and be visually inspected page by page before publication.

The committed PDF will be regenerated only after source and citation QA pass.

## 12. Quality assurance

### Content checks

- No use of “fourth primary instinct,” “hard-wired negotiate instinct,” “reptilian brain,” “grand unified theory,” or claims of validation unsupported by evidence.
- No claim that negotiation is necessarily prosocial, ethical, or win-win.
- No neuroanatomical or hormonal specificity beyond cited evidence.
- Polyvagal Theory is explicitly contested and non-essential.
- Cultural examples are not counted as biological evidence.
- The trading anecdote is not called validation.
- Limitations and falsification criteria are present.

### Citation checks

- Every bibliography key used in the paper exists.
- Every bibliography entry is cited or intentionally retained for a companion document.
- DOI/URL/title/author/year fields are checked against primary or publisher sources.
- No placeholder domains such as `example.com` remain.
- No popular-source citation supports a scientific proposition.

### Repository checks

- All README links resolve to committed paths.
- No stale filenames remain in links.
- Markdown heading hierarchy is valid.
- `CITATION.cff` parses as YAML.
- `references.bib` parses with a BibTeX parser.
- Build instructions are complete.
- The PDF opens, renders, and agrees with the source.

## 13. Change and publication workflow

1. Complete all work on `revision/agency-meta-response`.
2. Commit in coherent groups: framework, evidence/references, companion docs, repository metadata, PDF/build, final QA.
3. Open a pull request into `main` with a detailed change summary and explicit limitations.
4. Do not merge without Daniel Demure's explicit approval.
5. After merge, change the repository default branch to `main`, remove `initial-upload` when safe, create a preprint release, and optionally archive that release with Zenodo.

## 14. Acceptance criteria

The revision is complete when:

- the central claim is consistently a higher-order agency-preserving meta-response, not a proven fourth biological reflex;
- strict, intrapersonal, and existential uses are clearly separated;
- boundary conditions and disconfirmation criteria are explicit;
- at least four testable predictions and a staged research programme are included;
- Hexure is separated from the evidentiary core;
- weak reference material is replaced with a defensible scholarly bibliography;
- the README, citation, licence, review guidance, source paper, companion documents, build script, and PDF agree with one another;
- automated syntax/link/citation checks pass where feasible;
- the PDF passes visual inspection;
- and a reviewable pull request exists without being merged.
